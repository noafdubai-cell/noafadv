Quick Reference Guide

Professional Recommendations for Optimum Vehicle Wrap Performance

🧽

Surface Preparation

Ensure the application surface is clean, dry, and free from dust, grease, wax, silicone, and other contaminants before installation.

🎯

Film Selection

Select the Right Film:
• Cast – Recommended for full wraps, complex curves, deep recesses, and long-term applications.
• Polymeric – Ideal for flat surfaces, gentle curves, and medium-term graphics.

🌡️

Temperature

The recommended application temperature is minimum +10°C / +50°F. Ideal wrap bay ambient temperature is 15°C–25°C.

↔️

Stretch Control

Stretch the film only as required. Overstretching can reduce dimensional stability and long-term performance.

🔥

Post Heating

The temperature should be between 90°C and 110°C; check with the laser thermometer. Permanently locks vinyl shape memory.

⏱️

Lamination

Leave a drying time of at least 24 hours before laminating, cutting or applying it. Touch dry time is typically 10 minutes.

🌡️ Machine Temperature Suggestion: Set lamination machine to 40°C to 50°C (heat-assist eliminates silvering and accelerates adhesive wet-out).
✂️

Edge Finishing

Apply firm pressure along all edges and trim lines to ensure complete adhesion and prevent lifting over time. Leave a 2–3mm wrap margin.

⏳

Cure Time

Avoid washing or exposing the wrapped vehicle to harsh weather conditions for at least 24–48 hours after installation. (1 week before full wash).

📦

Storage

Store rolls vertically in their original packaging in a cool, dry environment (15°C–25°C, relative humidity 30%–70%) away from direct sunlight.

SEMI CURVE VINYL FILM — Professional Application Guide

POLYMERIC FILMS: Engineered for Reliable Performance on Flat & Gently Curved Surfaces

Professional Installation for Superior Performance: Semi-Curve Vinyl is specially developed for vehicle graphics and applications involving slight to moderate curves, contours, and recessed areas. With proper surface preparation and installation techniques, it provides reliable adhesion and an attractive appearance across various vehicle applications.

Suitable Application Surfaces

Surface Compatibility Guide
✔ Recommended Surfaces ✖ Not Recommended Surfaces
Painted vehicle panels Textured or rough surfaces
Glass surfaces, PVC, Melamine Acrylic painted surfaces
Steel & Aluminium Unpainted bumpers and plastic trims

SURFACE SUITABILITY

Before installation, carefully assess the vehicle surface and identify the level of curvature:

Flat & Slightly Curved Surfaces

Performs effectively on flat panels and gently curved vehicle areas, providing a smooth and consistent finish.

Moderate Curves

The material can be applied to moderately contoured surfaces when installed using the correct application technique and controlled heating.

Recessed Areas

For shallow recessed sections, the film should be carefully positioned and applied gradually to minimise tension and ensure proper adhesion.

Complex Curves Warning

For deep recesses, sharp compound curves, extreme channels, and highly three-dimensional surfaces, a more conformable Cast Vinyl may be the preferred solution.

SURFACE PREPARATION

Proper preparation is essential to achieve optimum adhesion and long-term performance. A perfectly prepared surface is the foundation of every successful installation:

  • ✔ Inspect the surface condition
  • ✔ Ensure paint is fully cured
  • ✔ Remove dust, grease, wax, silicone, tar and contaminants
  • ✔ Surface must be clean, dry and smooth

A properly prepared surface helps ensure consistent adhesion and reduces the risk of lifting, edge failure, or contamination beneath the film.

Ideal Storage Conditions

To maintain the quality of the film:

  • • Store between 15°C – 25°C
  • • Relative humidity: 30% – 70%
Key Pre-Installation Checks: Paint Curing

Fresh paint should cure completely before wrapping:
• Minimum 7 days at 25°C
• Air-dried automotive paint may require up to one month

APPLICATION TECHNIQUE (5 Key Steps)

1
Position the Graphic: Carefully align and position the vinyl before beginning the application.
2
Apply Gradually: Apply the film progressively using controlled pressure. Avoid forcing large areas of material onto the surface at one time.
3
Work from Flat Areas Towards Curves: Begin application on the flatter sections and gradually work towards the curved areas. This helps control the film and reduces unnecessary tension.
4
Use Controlled Heat: Heat may be used where necessary to improve flexibility and assist the film in conforming to moderate curves.
5
Avoid Excessive Stretching: Excessive stretching can create stress within the film and may increase the risk of lifting or shrinkage over time.
Curved Surfaces

When applying Semi-Curve Vinyl to curved areas, correct tension management is essential. Allow the film to follow the natural contour:

  • • Do not overstretch material & apply heat only where required.
  • • Avoid concentrating excessive tension in one area.
  • • Ensure film is secured along edges and transitions.
Edge & Finishing

Inspect all edges and finishing areas carefully:

  • • All edges firmly adhered; no air bubbles or wrinkles remain.
  • • Excess material trimmed cleanly; corners secured.
  • • Pay special attention to areas exposed to wind, water, washing, and vehicle movement.
Post-Application Inspection

Conduct a complete vehicle inspection covering:

  • • Curved sections & recessed areas.
  • • Panel gaps & heated sections.
  • • Sections subjected to stretching or tension.
  • • Correct any wrinkles, trapped air or lifting before release.
Post Heating — The Key to Long-Term Performance

Post heating is an essential stage of every professional installation. By reheating stretched areas to the recommended temperature of 90°C – 110°C, the film becomes permanently stabilised, significantly reducing shrinkage and edge lifting while improving long-term durability.

Vinyl Removal Protocol

Gradual heating softens the adhesive, allowing the film to be removed cleanly while minimising adhesive residue and protecting the underlying substrate.

  • • Heat the film to approximately 60°C
  • • Peel slowly at a 70°–80° angle

Care & Maintenance

  • ✔ Wait 48 hours before washing
  • ✔ Use pH-neutral automotive shampoo
  • ✔ Soft cloth or moderate automatic wash
  • ✔ Pressure washer at least 50 cm away
  • ✖ Avoid solvents, strong chemicals, abrasives

Lamination Tips

  • • Allow prints to dry completely before lamination
  • • Typical touch dry time: 10 minutes
  • • Recommended curing before lamination: 24 hours
  • • Lamination Machine Temperature: Suggestion 40°C to 50°C (heat-assisted warm roller prevents silvering, softens adhesive for instant wet-out, and eliminates micro air channels)
  • Proper curing improves solvent evaporation and ensures better performance.

Professional Installation Tips

  • ✔ Always install on a clean, dry surface
  • ✔ Never overstretch the film
  • ✔ Use a felt-edge squeegee for scratch-free finish
  • ✔ Reheat all stretched areas after installation
  • ✔ Avoid application on unpainted plastic trims
  • ✔ Perform adhesion and compatibility tests

Choose the Right Material. Apply with Precision. Deliver a Professional Finish.

Cast Film Series

CAST VINYL FILM — Professional Application Guide

CAST FILMS: Designed for complex surfaces. Engineered for precision.

Cast Vinyl is a premium vehicle wrapping material designed for highly curved, contoured, recessed, and three-dimensional surfaces. Its exceptional conformability allows the film to adapt smoothly to complex vehicle shapes while maintaining a professional and seamless appearance.

Flat & Slightly Curved Surfaces

Cast Vinyl can be applied smoothly to flat and gently curved surfaces while maintaining a consistent and professional finish.

Complex Curves

The material is designed to conform effectively around complex curves and contoured vehicle sections.

Deep Recesses & Channels

Suitable for application into deep recesses and channels when installed using the correct technique, controlled heat, and proper tension management.

Compound Curves

For surfaces that curve in multiple directions, it provides the flexibility required to achieve a smooth and uniform application.

SURFACE PREPARATION FOR CAST WRAPS

Proper surface preparation is essential for achieving optimum adhesion and long-term performance. Before application:

✔ Ensure the vehicle surface is thoroughly cleaned and completely dry.
✔ Remove dust, dirt, grease, wax, silicone, and other contaminants.
✔ Pay special attention to deep recesses, panel gaps, edges, trims, and difficult-to-reach areas.
✔ Ensure all cleaning residues have been completely removed.

A properly prepared surface helps achieve consistent adhesion and reduces the risk of lifting, edge failure, and contamination.

APPLICATION TECHNIQUE — CAST VINYL

1
Position the Film: Carefully align and position the vinyl before beginning the application. Ensure sufficient material is available to cover complex curves, recesses, and contoured areas without creating unnecessary tension.
2
Begin with Stable Areas: Where possible, start from a flat or stable section of the panel and gradually work towards more complex curves and recessed areas.
3
Apply with Controlled Pressure: Use smooth and consistent pressure during application. Avoid forcing the material into difficult areas too quickly.
4
Use Heat to Assist Conformability: Controlled heat can be used to increase the flexibility of the film and assist it in conforming to complex vehicle surfaces. Heat should always be applied carefully and evenly to avoid overheating the material.
5
Manage Tension Carefully: Although Cast Vinyl is highly conformable, excessive stretching should still be avoided. The film should be allowed to conform naturally wherever possible. Proper tension management is essential around deep recesses, sharp contours, and compound curves.

Application on Complex Curves

Cast Vinyl adapts to challenging shapes. When working on complex curves:

  • • Apply the material gradually.
  • • Use controlled heat where required.
  • • Avoid excessive stretching in one direction.
  • • Distribute tension evenly across the surface.
  • • Allow the film to relax before finalising difficult areas.

Application in Deep Recesses & Channels

Deep recessed areas require special attention during installation:

  • • Apply the film progressively into the recess without forcing or overstretching.
  • • Avoid bridging the material across deep channels.
  • • Minimise excessive stretching.
  • • Ensure the material is properly seated within the recessed area.
  • • Check for tension or stress after application.

Edge & Finishing Techniques

Once the vinyl has been applied, carefully complete all trimming and finishing work:

• All edges are firmly adhered and corners are properly secured.
• Excess material is trimmed cleanly.
• Recessed areas are fully checked; no air bubbles or wrinkles remain.
• High-tension areas are inspected carefully.
• Panel gaps and transitions are properly finished. Areas exposed to wind, water, regular washing, or frequent contact should receive particular attention during final inspection.
Critical Procedure Commercial Trucks & Trailers

APPLICATION OVER RIVETS

When encountering rivets on truck bodies and commercial trailer panels, follow this exact temperature-calibrated sequence:

Step 1 · Gentle Preheat
40°C – 50°C

Gently heat the film to soften it before forming around the rivet head.

Step 2 · Circumferential Pressure
Full 360° Seal

Apply sufficient, even pressure around the complete rivet surface using rivet brush/tool.

Step 3 · Post-Heat Rivet
90°C – 110°C

Post-heat each rivet to 90°C–110°C to lock adhesion and eliminate tenting.

✔ Designed For Complex Vehicle Surfaces

Door handles, curved body panels, recessed areas, convex sections, riveted surfaces, seams and overlaps, highly stretched areas.

✖ Application Should Be Avoided On

Window seals, body panel seals, unpainted trims & unpainted plastic bumpers.

The Importance of Post-Heating

After application, areas that have undergone significant stretching or deformation should be heated to 90°C to 110°C. Post-heating helps reduce the shape memory of the vinyl and allows the pressure-sensitive adhesive to bond more effectively. Particularly important for: concave, convex surfaces, rivets, deep recesses, edges and complex contours.

Cleaning and Maintenance

The film should not be cleaned during the first: 48 hours after application, as early cleaning may affect adhesive strength.

4-Step Removal Procedure

  1. 1. Begin from a corner of the graphic, heat the film to approximately 60°C.
  2. 2. Carefully lift the film without damaging the substrate.
  3. 3. Remove the film gradually while maintaining an angle of approximately 70° to 80°.
  4. 4. Continue heating small sections while peeling slowly; maintain controlled heat, pulling angle, and speed.

Part IV · Standardized Hands-On Workflow Shop Floor Execution

13-Stage Operation Manual

This section preserves the complete, verified, standardized hands-on workshop operations step-by-step — from vehicle intake and deep clay wash to protective film removal, trimming, and customer handover.

1. Abstract

Car wrapping is a process that has high requirements for techniques and procedures. Standardized operations can not only ensure the aesthetics and durability of the film application effectives, but also avoid damage to the vehicle. This instruction manual is suitable for the application of various types of automotive film.

2. Preparation

Adequate preparation is the foundation for ensuring the smooth progress of the film application. The specific details are as follows:

Environment preparation

Confirm that the wrap shop is clean, dust-free and well-lit. Maintain the temperature at 15–25°C and avoid wrapping in windy, humid or high-temperature environment.

Vehicle Pre-inspection

Check the vehicle condition together with the customer. Record paint defects, scratches, etc., and take photos for fixing to avoid subsequent disputes.

Tool Preparation & Display

Prepare all tools required for car wrapping, including scrapers, film-cutting knives, spray bottles, heat guns, masking tape, masking film, microfiber towels, etc.

Material Preparation

Verify the item code and color of the film. Check the film for quality issues such as air bubbles, scratches, color differences, etc.

3 & 4. Vehicle Clean & Drying

Cleaning is crucial to ensure the quality of car wrapping, and the surface to be filmed must be absolutely clean. The specific steps are as follows:

1

Rinse the vehicle surface with a high-pressure water gun to remove loose dust and large particle impurities.

2

Spray foam cleaner and wipe the vehicle body with a soft sponge or car wash mitt, focusing on cleaning seams, edges and tight areas.

3

Rinse off the foam with clean water, then use a dedicated clay bar to remove stubborn stains such as iron deposits and tar from the paint surface.

Key point: Drying & Moisture Evaporation
  • • Thoroughly dry the vehicle body with a clean microfiber towel, taking care to avoid leaving water spots.
  • • After washing, allow the vehicle to air dry in the sun for 30 minutes. Alternatively, use an air blower to remove residual water droplets from seams and hard-to-reach areas.

5. Surface Dust and Contaminant Removal

Use a high-pressure air gun to blow off dust and particles from the film surface and the back of release liner.

Avoid peeling off the adhesive protective film of the material during the cleaning process.

If the edge of the film's protective layer lifts, gently secure it with masking tape.

6. Removal & Protection of Accessories

To protect non-wrapping areas from damage and create an unobstructed environment for film application, the following operations are required:

Protection

Use masking tape and masking film to tightly protect non-wrapping areas such as headlights, taillights, painted parts, chrome trim kits, and glass edges. Ensure the edges fit tightly with no unprotected areas left.

Removal of Accessories

Dismantle parts that may hinder car wrapping, including large and small body emblems, door handles, and antennas. Before removal, confirm the type of fasteners, use dedicated tools to avoid damage caused by brute force, and properly store the removed parts.

7. Measurement & Film Cutting

Accurate measurement and cutting lay the foundation for subsequent wrapping. The specific methods are as follows:

1

Use a tape measure to accurately measure the dimensions of the area to be filmed, and perform cutting on a clean film-cutting table.

2

When cutting the film, the size of the film should be 5–10mm larger than the actual coverage area to leave a margin for edge wrapping.

3

Use a professional film-cutting knife and a straightedge to ensure the cut is flat and smooth, avoiding burrs or cutting deviations.

Hood Wrapping Center-Line Method

• Locate the center line of the hood, and use a professional film-cutting knife or low-tack tape to score a center line mark on the film's backing paper.
• Perform the application separately on the left and right sides. First peel off a small section of backing paper, align it with the center line and fix it in place.
• Then slowly peel off the remaining backing paper, using a squeegee to smooth out the film at the same time, avoiding adhesion or misalignment.

Side Panel Application Single vs Multi-Person

Single-Person: Position film using front or rear bumper as reference point. Partially remove liner, adhere a small section, secure remainder with magnetic positioning pins, and proceed front-to-rear or vice versa.
Multi-Person: Completely remove release liner. Stretch film evenly across vehicle contours for positioning (pre-mark with positioning tape). Once positioned, squeegee from panel center outward to edges.

8. Heat Stretching and Molding

Heat the film and stretch it to ensure it perfectly fits the complex curved surfaces of the vehicle body. Key points:

Use a heat gun together with a squeegee. Set an appropriate heat gun temperature based on the PVC film material.

When heating, move the heat gun at a constant speed, keeping it 10–15cm away from the film surface. After heating, let it cool for a few seconds.

Use your palm or a squeegee to press the film, ensuring it closely adheres to the curved surface of the vehicle body.

Note that excessively high temperature or prolonged heating will cause the film to deform, char, or damage the vehicle's paint finish.

9. Squeegee the Film

The purpose of squeegeeing the film is to completely expel moisture and air bubbles between the film and the paint surface:

While slowly peeling off the backing paper, use a squeegee to smooth the film, working sequentially from the center to the sides.

Keep the squeegee at an approximately 30-degree angle to the film surface, maintaining even and steady pressure to avoid wrinkles, air bubbles, or film adhesion.

For larger film pieces, we recommend two people to cooperate: one person peels off the backing paper, and the other squeegees the film.

10. Film Inspection & Trim off Edges

Before edge finishing and securing, carefully inspect the film surface:

01 · Under Sufficient Light

Check the film surface for defects such as air bubbles, water bubbles, dirt spots, and scratches.

02 · Small Air Bubbles

Resolve them by pricking to release air or re-squeegee the film.

03 · Dirt Spots

If location allows, lift film edge and use dedicated cleaning solution with tape to stick out impurities. If defects cannot be repaired, replace with new film without hesitation.

Trim off Edges

Trim off the excess film edges to ensure they fit perfectly with vehicle body lines. Perform edge trimming only after confirming film is fully adhered, shaped, and all water squeezed out. Use a new, sharp utility knife to cut along vehicle body gaps or protective tape edges. Leave a 2–3mm margin for edge wrapping, ensuring trimmed edges are neat and smooth.

11. Peel off Protective Film & Finish Edges

Peel off Protective Film: Before edge finishing, peel off the scratch-resistant protective film from the material surface to facilitate easier adhesion during edge finishing.

Finish Edges

Push the reserved edge-wrapping film into the gaps to prevent edge lifting and ensure long-term stability:

  • • Use a small squeegee or dedicated edge-finishing tool to heat the film edge and soften it.
  • • Gently push the softened film edge into the gap with the tool.
  • • Keep movements gentle to avoid damaging the vehicle paint or weatherstripping.

12. Reinstall All Removed Accessories

After wrapping is completed, perform the part reset operation:

Remove Masking: Carefully peel off masking tape and masking film that protected headlights, painted parts, chrome trim kits, and avoid leaving adhesive residue.
Reinstall Accessories: Reinstall removed accessories such as emblems and door handles, ensuring they are securely installed, correctly positioned, and consistent with original vehicle appearance.

13. Inspection & Notice

Conduct a final comprehensive inspection to ensure wrapping quality:

✔ Check for color gaps and exposed paint on seams or edges.
✔ Inspect the film surface for dirt spots and unexpelled air bubbles.
✔ Confirm all edges are smooth, secure, and free from lifting.
✔ Verify that all accessories are properly and securely installed.
Customer Handover: Washing Rules

Do not wash the vehicle within one week after film application, and avoid direct high-pressure water spray on the film edges.

SAFETY & OPERATOR NOTICE

During the car wrapping process, pay attention to protecting the vehicle to avoid scratching the paint surface or damaging parts with tools. Operators must wear gloves to prevent hangnails from being left on the film or paint surface. For films of different materials, parameters such as construction temperature and stretching force vary. Strictly follow the film's product instructions for operation.
